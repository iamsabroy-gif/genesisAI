* **5-Speed Manual (MT)**: Features short, crisp throws with a feather-light clutch action. The low-end torque allows smooth pulling from as low as 1,200 rpm without engine knocking, minimizing gear shifts in stop-and-go city traffic.
* **5-Speed Smart Auto (AMT)**: Hyundai’s electric-actuator-driven automated manual transmission is notably smoother than early-generation pneumatic AMTs. * **Suspension Geometry**:
  * *Front*: MacPherson Strut with coil springs and anti-roll bar.

  * *Ride Tuning*: Tuned on the pliant, absorbent side. Small potholes and urban expansion joints are rounded off effortlessly; high-speed vertical pitch is well controlled up to 100 km/h.
